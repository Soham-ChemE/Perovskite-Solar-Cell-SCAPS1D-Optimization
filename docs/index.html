A SCAPS-1D device-physics study of an FTO / ZnSe / CdTe thin-film cell: ZnSe replaces the CdS window, then ten electron-transport layers, nine back-contact metals, four layer thicknesses and temperature are swept. The lead-free double perovskite Cs₂BiAgI₆ with a nickel contact gives a simulated 27.99 %. A ceiling for this parameter set, not a fabricated cell.

01 · Electron-transport layer screening

Two layers reach the absorber's current limit. Two collapse to nothing.

Each ETL was swept from 0.4 to 2.0 µm with the rest of the stack fixed (FTO 0.4 / ZnSe 0.025 / CdTe 3 µm / Ni). Cs₂BiAgI₆ and C₆₀ share near-ideal fill factors and almost the full 26 mA/cm² a 1.5 eV absorber can deliver under AM 1.5G; both have the same electron affinity as CdTe, so there is no conduction-band step. PCBM and WS₂ keep a normal VOC and fill factor but lose the current, so the loss is in collection, not the junction.

02 · Layer thickness and temperature

The absorber gains current with thickness, the front contact loses it, the buffer is inert.

With Cs₂BiAgI₆ selected, each layer was swept in turn. Step through the sweeps: the J-V curve and the four metrics come straight from the SCAPS export for that point.

CdTe absorber: 27.23 → 27.85 %

JSC rises with thickness as more photons are absorbed; VOC falls slightly with more bulk recombination. The optimum stack uses 3 µm.

FTO front contact: 27.89 → 26.96 %

Thinner is better. A thicker transparent conductor is a parasitic absorber in front of the junction.

ZnSe buffer: flat at 27.8 %

Performance moves by 0.02 points across 0.4 to 2.0 µm. The 2.9 eV window passes the spectrum to the absorber whatever its thickness; 0.025 µm is used.

Cs₂BiAgI₆ ETL: 27.81 → 27.99 %

A 0.18-point rise across the sweep. The 2 µm choice is the end of the swept range, not a resolved optimum, and the page says so.

Temperature: 300 → 400 K

JSC does not move. VOC falls at −1.66 mV/K as the dark saturation current rises, and efficiency derates at −0.049 points per kelvin, about −0.18 %/K relative, milder than crystalline silicon's −0.35 to −0.45 %/K.

03 · Back contact

Efficiency tracks the metal work function until the hole barrier closes.

MetalWork function (eV)Hole barrier ≈ 5.4 − φ (eV)PCE (%)

For a p-type absorber the metal has to line up with the valence band. In this parameter set the CdTe valence-band edge sits at χ + Eg = 3.9 + 1.5 = 5.4 eV below vacuum, so a metal with a smaller work function leaves a hole barrier of roughly 5.4 − φ: about 1.1 eV for aluminium, 0.2 eV for tungsten, none for nickel and above. Pt and Se edge out Ni by under half a point but are respectively scarce and toxic, so Ni is the practical optimum.

04 · What the study does and does not show

A ranking you can trust more than the absolute number.

It shows

which of ten candidate layers and nine metals are compatible with a ZnSe/CdTe junction in a drift-diffusion model, how each layer's thickness trades current against voltage, and how the optimum stack derates with temperature.

It does not show

an achievable cell efficiency. SCAPS-1D is one-dimensional and planar: no grain boundaries, no texture or anti-reflection design beyond the front glass, negligible series resistance, only the interface defects that were entered. 27.99 % is a ceiling for this parameter set, several points above the record for any CdTe device. Cs₂BiAgI₆ has not been demonstrated as an ETL on CdTe experimentally; the study is a motivation for trying it.

Beyond the simulation

The project's hardware concept, a detachable spiral thin-film panel with 17.9 % more active area than a planar panel of the same footprint, holds two granted Indian design registrations (434427-001, October 2024; 459195-001, May 2025) with one utility application under review. The work received the Best Poster Award at the 61st Annual Convention of Chemists (ACC 2024).
